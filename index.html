your phone’s RAM

When you open an app, the phone must find a free block of RAM big enough. Worst Fit always picks the largest free block, then splits off what’s left. Launch apps and watch which hole gets chosen.

What happened

    1. Scan every hole

    The memory manager looks at all free blocks, not just the first one that fits.

    2. Take the biggest

    The largest hole wins. The app gets the start of it, and the rest stays free as a new, smaller hole.

    3. Closing merges holes

    When an app closes, its block turns free and joins any free neighbours into one bigger hole.

    Why it can fail

    Worst Fit keeps leaving medium-sized leftovers. Eventually a big app may not fit even when total free RAM is enough. That is external fragmentation.
